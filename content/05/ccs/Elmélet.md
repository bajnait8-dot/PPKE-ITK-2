> [!question]- Signal
> A (physical) quantity, which depends on time, space or other independent variables. 

> [!question]- System
>  A physical or logical device that performs operations on signals. (Processes input signals, and generates output signals.)

> [!question]- Laplace-transform
> $$\mathcal{L}\{f\}(s)=\int_0^\infty f(t)e^{-st}dt,\quad f\in V, s\in\mathbb{C}$$

> [!question]- Dirac-delta
> $$\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(t)\delta(t)dt=f(0)$$

> [!question]- Time-shifting
> $$T_ax(t)=x(t-a)\quad\forall t\in\mathbb{R}_0^+,a\in\mathbb{R}$$

> [!question]- Convolution
> $$(x*y)(t)=\int_0^tx(T)y(t-T)dT\quad\forall t\geq0$$
